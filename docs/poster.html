Ultra-bold 1:2 layout pairing high-contrast digital hours with stacked period indicators (PG/PTG/MLM) and localized Gregorian date.

B. Weather & Haze Pill

Directly beneath the clock. One-click D-Pad access displaying live temperature, weather icon, and official APIMS Air Pollutant Index.

02. macOS Apple Weather Bento Dashboard

WeatherActivity

Atmospheric Bento & Dynamic Weather Open-Meteo & APIMS

  • • Animated Atmosphere: Dynamic particle & sky background transitions automatically (sunny rays, falling rain, thunderstorm, drifting clouds, stars).
  • • Doppler Rain Radar: Live high-resolution radar precipitation overlay with interactive regional switching.
  • • 10-Day Bento Forecast: Pixel-perfect horizontal hourly pills and 10-day predictive temperature bars with zero baseline bounce.

03. 16:9 Azan & Solat Floating Settings

AzanSettingsDialog

Harmonious Prayer Architecture JAKIM E-Solat Sync

  • • 16:9 Floating Modal: Frosted translucent dialog displaying prayer toggles, audio selections, and live test previews.
  • • Individual Prayer Controls: Dedicated ON/OFF toggle switches for Subuh, Syuruk, Zohor, Asar, Maghrib, and Isyak.
  • • Audio Engine & Preview: Instant Azan preview button with As-Syakirin KLCC, Makkah, and Madinah audio presets.

04. StandBy Mode & OLED Protection

ClockDream & Power Lifecycle

Intelligent Idle & Power Lifecycle 10m Idle • 2h Standby

  • • 10-Min Inactivity: Transitions to OLED-safe burn-in protected StandBy screensaver with 110sp digits.
  • • Cultural Harmony: Islamic Hijri date (12 Jamadilawal 1448H) and next prayer countdown capsule.
  • • 2-Hour Auto Power-Off: System puts TV into deep sleep standby via Accessibility Service (GLOBAL_ACTION_LOCK_SCREEN).

05. 4K Malaysian Wallpapers & Mobile QR Upload

WallpaperManager

Curated Landscapes & Personal Memories 14 States • Wi-Fi Server

  • • 14 Malaysian States: Dedicated 4K Pexels collections highlighting iconic state landmarks (Terengganu Drawbridge, KLCC, Gunung Kinabalu).
  • • Phone QR Upload: On-device Wi-Fi upload server allows instant photo transfers from any smartphone without cloud accounts.
  • • Clean Photo View: Personal uploaded photos automatically hide overlay shelves for an unobstructed living room art gallery.
